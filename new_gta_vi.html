Welcome to Vice City, Leonida

Grand Theft Auto VI returns players to the iconic Vice City—reimagined for a modern era within the broader fictional state of Leonida (inspired by Florida). From luxury beachfront high-rises and vibrant nightlife districts to the wild, alligator-filled Grassrivers and coastal keys, every corner of the map pulses with chaotic energy, viral social media culture, and systemic open-world life.

Built around a high-risk partnership, the narrative follows dual protagonists navigating a web of heists, law enforcement pressure, and betrayal where trust is the only currency that matters.
